Work

Film2026

Which Way (2026)

A psychological musical drama about a Lagos mechanic and a family that sings what it cannot say. I co-wrote the script with director Jide Okeke and wrote the lyrics for most of the songs. Africa premiere at AFRIFF 2026, after winning Best Director and Best Cinematography at the Portugal Indie Film Festival 2026.

Directed by Jide Okeke · 113 minutes · Nigeria / United Kingdom

Radio2020

How You See Am (2020)

A six episode radio drama in Pidgin English that follows an average Nigerian family trying to make the right choice of cooking oil. It starts in the kitchen, but the stakes reach well beyond it, because the wrong oil costs a family in its health, its money and its future. I wrote it for Devon King's Oil in 2020, and it was scheduled on radio stations across the country.

Animation2022

How You See Am (animated series) (2022)

An animated series adapted from my Pidgin English radio scripts and released on Devon King's YouTube channel. It was translated into Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa. According to the agency, Onewildcard, the campaign reached 4.9 million people, with 3.5 million views and 900,000 engagements. The adaptation was shortlisted at the 2023 Pitcher Awards (Digital, Social Media Campaigns).

Stage2026

Waiting with the Madisons (2026)

A comic musical satire about Bode and Funmi Madison, a married couple who spend one long day waiting for a therapist and a lawyer to settle their divorce, while fighting over every necessary and unnecessary thing. Underneath the quarrelling is a marriage neither of them can quite leave. I wrote the script and the lyrics and directed it. Premiered at the Lagos Fringe Festival on 7 November 2026, at the J. Randle Centre, Onikan.

About

I’m drawn to stories that shift how we see ourselves and question what we take for granted, and I tell them in whatever medium serves them best: film, stage, radio, animation.

I am a narrative screenwriter and stage director with a passion for high-stakes human drama and subtle magical realism. Over the last decade, I have written for international studios, independent theater companies, and audio drama networks.

I am a scriptwriter at AfriBold Pictures, and the founder and senior copywriter at PitchFC, a solo creative agency built on the craft of persuasion. I write for screen, stage, radio, animation and TV, and I direct for the stage.

My stories come out of Lagos. They explore the culture and life of Nigeria, and they stay relevant to audiences around the world.
